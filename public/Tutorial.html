Welcome to Vitaminarian

Vitaminarian is a customized nutrition tracking application that helps you log foods and meals, visualize macronutrient and micronutrient intake, and manage your personal nutrition data through CSV files.

Getting Started

When you first open the application, you will see a disclaimer popup. Please read it carefully. The app loads with a default Food Info.csv file containing nutritional data for many common foods. The Meals.csv and Food Log.csv files start empty.

Upload and Download Data

Use the Upload Data and Download Data buttons to manage your CSV files:

  • Food Info.csv — Contains food names, serving sizes, calories, and nutrient values per serving, plus a Daily Value row with your daily intake goals.
  • Meals.csv — Defines composite meals made from foods or other meals.
  • Food Log.csv — Records what you ate each day.

When uploading, you can choose to Merge with existing or Replace existing data. Files are validated before and after upload.

Logging Foods

In the Food Log Entry section:

  1. Select the date you want to log for using the date picker.
  2. Search for a food or meal in the Item dropdown.
  3. Enter a positive quantity and select a unit (grams or servings for foods; servings only for meals).
  4. Click Add to record the entry.

Nutrient Breakdown

The Nutrient Breakdown section shows detailed nutrition information:

  • Select Food Log to see totals for everything logged on a specific date.
  • Select Specific Item to analyze a single food or meal at a given quantity.

The dashboard includes:

  • Food Breakdown Tree — Expandable tree showing how meals decompose into individual foods.
  • Calorie Progress Bar — Progress toward your daily calorie goal.
  • Macronutrient Dashboard — Pie chart and progress bars for fat, carbohydrates, protein, and sub-nutrients.
  • Micronutrient Dashboard — Progress bars for vitamins, minerals, and other micronutrients.

Tips

  • Changing the food log date automatically updates the nutrient breakdown to show that day's food log.
  • Download your data regularly to keep backups.
  • Food and meal names must be unique across both files.
